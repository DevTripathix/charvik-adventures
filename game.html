Young explorer Charvik has washed ashore on Mistara, where spirit orbs hum in the air and the Golden Idol sleeps beyond the volcano. Cross the beach, pierce the jungle, climb Emberpeak — and awaken the powers within.

HOW TO PLAY

Survive the island · gather the Idol
MOVE← → or A D — run across the island
